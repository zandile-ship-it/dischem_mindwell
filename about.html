About MindWell

MindWell Youth Support is a new division under Dis-Chem designed to provide affordable mental wellness services and products for South African youth.

Our mission is to make emotional support accessible to everyone, especially students and young adults who may be facing financial challenges. We offer a range of services including virtual counselling, anonymous peer communities, and guided meditation sessions, as well as a curated selection of wellness products.

We believe that mental wellness is just as important as physical health, and we are committed to supporting the emotional well-being of our youth. By partnering with qualified professionals and leveraging technology, we aim to create a safe and supportive space for young people to thrive.
